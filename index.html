03 / IDEA IN PROGRESS

An early question I want to turn into an experiment.

A research direction should become more precise through reading, implementation, and evidence. GOLPO is currently an evolving idea, not a completed framework.

GOLPO · Game-theoretic Oversight and Learning Protocol

Can an agent balance what it wants to achieve with what it must avoid?

I am exploring whether constrained decision-making and game-theoretic safety analysis can help an autonomous agent balance task performance with harm avoidance. The immediate goal is to narrow the question, study the relevant foundations, and build a small, reproducible baseline experiment.

Safe RLGame theoryConstrained optimizationAgent safety
Current stage · Formulating the question

What comes next?

Define a measurable safety objective, implement a baseline agent, choose evaluation metrics, and study the trade-off between task reward and risk.

No empirical findings are claimed at this stage.

04 / INTERACTIVE NOTE

A shorter path is not always the better path.

A small illustration of decision-making when progress and safety pull in different directions.

A simple planning problem

Should an agent take the shortest route, or avoid more risk?

Imagine an agent moving through an environment with hazardous cells. A route that minimizes distance may pass close to danger. Increasing the safety priority makes the planner more cautious, potentially adding steps to reduce risk exposure.

This is a hand-coded path-planning illustration — not a trained reinforcement-learning agent or a result from GOLPO.
